GT350 Hunter

Shelby GT350 & GT350R across the marketplaces

GT350 Hunter reads real listings by briefly opening each site's pre-filtered search in a background tab, letting it render, and scraping the results — then closing the tab. Matches feed the Market dashboard.

You'll see tabs open and close while it works. Some sites still show a consent or robot page; when one can't be read, use its Open ↗ link — the filters are already applied.
